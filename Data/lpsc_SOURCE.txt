Contents
- lpsc_600K / lpsc_1200K: 85 DFT MD snapshots each of a 416-atom Li6PS5Cl cell (energy, forces, stress).
- lpsc_test_md: 200 DFT MD snapshots used as the test set.
- lpsc_eos: 11 scaled cells of Li6PS5Cl with DFT energies (energy-volume curve; Materials Project-compatible PBE settings).
- sevennet_0_small_checkpoint: the small pretrained SevenNet model fine-tuned in class.
- lpsc_finetuned_checkpoint: that model fine-tuned on the Li6PS5Cl data, used in the post-class notebook if a student has no checkpoint of their own.
