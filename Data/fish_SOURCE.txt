The repository holds the code of Haesun Park's textbook 혼자 공부하는 머신러닝+딥러닝 (Hanbit Media).
The measurements are the fish catch data from Lake Laengelmavesi, Finland (159 fish of seven species),
submitted by Juha Puranen to the Journal of Statistics Education data archive.
The preclass notebook uses the 56 Perch rows.
